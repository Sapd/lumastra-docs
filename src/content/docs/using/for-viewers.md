Your administrator provides your account and server address, and decides which features
are available. An account age limit can hide titles from browse, search, and
recommendations; see [Parental controls & age ratings](/guides/parental-controls/).

## Browsing and search

**Browse → Library · A–Z** searches within your selected library and filters. Global
search covers all configured libraries and services. **Include descriptions** broadens
search beyond titles and people; a **+** after a result count means more results can
be loaded.

Browse URLs preserve your search, filters, sort, and layout, so you can bookmark a view.

Series posters in Library · A–Z count your unwatched **playable** episodes, including
specials. A checkmark means you've watched every currently available episode. Series
cards and details show season and episode counts when known; details distinguish the
total from episodes available to play and show your watch progress.

## Continue Watching

Resuming a series follows its saved watch marker, including progress from other devices.
If that takes you somewhere unexpected, a notice names the device and offers to jump
back. Finished series offer the next season or a rewatch.

Restore dismissed titles on **Hidden from Continue Watching** at `/my/hidden`.

## Recommendations and ratings

Ratings and watch history shape **For You**. Dismiss a “Because you watched” row if its
seed title isn't useful. Selecting multiple moods matches all of them; clearing the
selection returns to automatic recommendations.

The **Rate** card stack uses these gestures, which also update the title's star rating:

| Swipe | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Right | Like |
| Left | Dislike |
| Up | Love / hype |
| Down | Skip |

AI recommendations need an embedding provider configured by your administrator.

## Playlists

New playlists are private. **Sharing & members** manages invitations and access;
invited users must accept before a shared playlist appears for them.

Adding a series to a regular playlist expands its currently catalogued episodes. Adding
it to **Watchlist** saves one series entry instead.

Video playback starts at the first unchecked video. Playlist checkmarks are separate
from playback resume positions. Video navigation skips music entries; those entries
remain visible in the playlist drawer but cannot start there.

To reorder with a keyboard, focus an item's handle, press **Space**, use the arrow keys,
then press **Space** again. Collapse a series or season to move its episodes together.
The system Watchlist keeps its automatic order and cannot be renamed, shared, or deleted.

## Requesting titles

Requests ask your administrator to add a title that isn't available. The request form
shows any quota and lets you choose seasons. You'll receive a notification when the
title is playable. Requests require your administrator to enable them and configure Seerr.

## Watch Together

Start a party from the player's **Watch party** button and share its code or link.
A new party is **private**: only people who have the code or link can join. Choose to list
it if you want it to appear in the **Watch Parties** widget for others on the server.
Join through the widget (listed parties) or the shared link.

- Anyone can pause or resume for everyone.
- Only the host can seek, skip segments, change speed, or move to another title.
- The next episode waits at the start until someone presses Play.
- Leaving as a guest affects only you. Ending as host stops the party for everyone;
  a party also ends after the host has been gone for about 10 minutes.

On Apple TV, start from a title's **Watch Together** button or join by code from the
sidebar. Pressing Home keeps the party membership; use **Rejoin Party** when you return.
Pressing Back twice leaves the party, ending it for everyone if you are the host.

## Remote control

Use your phone or the web as a remote for your other devices. Start a title on the TV,
pause, seek, pick a track or send a message. You can also allow a household helper to
control your devices. See [Remote control](/using/remote-control/).

## Music, audiobooks, and Live TV

Music's **Smart Queue / Autoplay** adds recommendations as the queue runs out. Crossfade
is configured in the full-screen player's settings. For the Mixing Desk, taste stations,
and other discovery tools, see [Music discovery](/guides/music-discovery/).

Audiobook search stays within your selected shelf, language, and filters.
**Continue listening** covers all shelves. Live TV search includes all channels and
scheduled programmes, even when you're browsing Favorites. For a larger, simpler Live TV
screen with only your own numbered channels, see
[Live TV simple mode](/guides/live-tv-simple-mode/).

## Preferences

Audio/subtitle language, subtitle style, playback automation, and music crossfade settings
follow your account across browsers. Playback automation is under **Player → Quality →
Playback Settings**; see [Using the video player](/using/player/).

Widget order and visibility (**Settings → Home**), dock visibility, and
stream info visibility are saved per browser. Your administrator can also change the
homepage and available widgets for the whole server.

## Privacy

**Settings → Privacy** controls what other users can see:

| Setting | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Hide my streams** | Hides streaming and social activity; overrides the sharing settings below. |
| **Share audiobook activity** | Shares book progress, finished books, and shared listening. |
| **Share coarse listening activity** | Shares books listened to, rounded duration, and day; never exact times. |

Everything starts **off**: a new account hides its streams and shares no listening or
audiobook activity until you turn it on here. Accounts that never opened these settings
were switched to this private default when the server was upgraded; accounts that saved a
choice keep it.

Coarse listening activity is reciprocal: you can see others' activity only while sharing
your own. Your administrator defines the audience, usually everyone on the server or
people in the same group.

When audiobook sharing is enabled, hide one book by opening its **…** menu and
clicking the listening-sharing icon. Its tooltip shows whether your progress is
visible to others. Reviews are visible only within your Lumastra server.

## Notifications

New episodes and fulfilled requests appear in the notification bell and follow your
account across devices. Browser system notifications need permission. Notifications
aren't sent by email.

## MyAnimeList

Link your account under **Settings → Connections** to optionally sync anime ratings.
